Sign in

Open the project on odoo.sh, select the main branch and press Connect. You arrive signed in as the administrator. No password is published on this page.

Before you present

Three things to know

A new commit to main rebuilds the database and erases the demo. The Odoo.sh trial ends about 29 days after 26 November 2026. Set the display language and time zone to Manila before sharing your screen.

Demo scripts

Five stories, about fifteen minutes.

Each story matches one of MOL's asks. Follow them in order, or pick the ones the audience cares about.

Suggested order

From the simplest to the one that answers MOL's main question

1The fleet at a glance
2Renewals that warn you
3A driver's day
4The idling question
5A scanned contact
2 minutes

1 · The fleet at a glance

  1. Open Fleet. Ten vehicles appear as cards under "Registered".
  2. Point out the plate, model, driver and tags on each card.
  3. Filter by the tag "Executive car" to show three vehicles.
  4. Open NFA 1023 (Alphard). Show the buttons along the top: drivers, contracts, services, odometer, equipment.

"Every vehicle has one record, and everything about it hangs off that record."

3 minutes

2 · Renewals that warn you

  1. On the Fleet board, find the warning icons on NFA 1023 and NDC 7302 (due soon) and NBD 5518 (overdue).
  2. Open NBD 5518 and press Contracts. Its insurance expired on 20 November.
  3. Go to Fleet › Contracts and sort by expiration date to see the whole year.

"Nothing depends on someone remembering a date in a spreadsheet."

3 minutes

3 · A driver's day

  1. Open Attendances › Overview and switch to the list. Filter on Ramon Dela Cruz.
  2. Open one record. Show the check-in and check-out times and the location recorded with each.
  3. Open his vehicle, NFA 1023, and press Odometer. One reading per working day lines up with the attendance.
  4. Mention that the odometer photo is an addition TechNext would build; the demo shows the readings only.

"Two clock-ins a day give you hours and kilometres from a single action."

4 minutes

4 · The idling question

  1. Open vehicle NAH 6630 (mu-X, driver Ricardo Aquino) and press Odometer. Note the small daily distances.
  2. Open Attendances for Ricardo Aquino: 11 to 12 hour days on 5, 12, 13, 19 and 24 November.
  3. Open Fleet › Reporting › Costs and group by vehicle. NAH 6630's fuel is far above vehicles that drive more.
  4. Open its Services: a check of fuel consumption is already booked for 8 December.

"Long hours, few kilometres, high fuel. This is the pattern you asked how to find."

3 minutes

5 · A scanned contact

  1. Open Contacts and filter on the tag "Scanned card".
  2. Open Hiroshi Tanabe. Show the company, title, phone and email, and the note that it came from the card app.
  3. Use the action menu to Download (vCard), which a phone adds to its address book.
  4. Point out the phone number: with Phone installed it can be clicked to call. WhatsApp and SMS are offered from the same record. None is connected to a provider in the demo, so do not press send.

"The card is typed by nobody, and it belongs to MOL, not to one person's phone."

Optional

Extra · Equipment on board

  1. Open any vehicle tagged "Tablet fitted" and press Equipment.
  2. Show the tablet, dash camera and safety kit listed against the vehicle.
  3. Link this to asset management, which MOL's team plans to configure in phase 2.

"An audit of the vehicle is an audit of what is inside it."

How it was built

From an empty build to the MOL demo.

Build steps

All done through Odoo's programming interface with an administrator key

CleanSample users and 37 sample contacts deleted
Switch offSample-data loading disabled before any app was installed
CompanyRenamed to MOL, Makati, pesos
InstallFleet, Attendances, Employees, Contacts, Maintenance, Time Off, CRM, Phone, WhatsApp
LoadFictional MOL records created

To rebuild after a reset

  • Connect to the new build and create an administrator API key.
  • Run the three steps again: clean, install, load. TechNext keeps the loading script.
  • The build address changes if the branch is rebuilt, so update the link on this page.

To keep it permanently

  • Move the branch to Production on Odoo.sh. Production databases are not rebuilt on each commit and start without sample data.
  • A project has one production database, so this one would become the demo.
  • The trial needs a paid subscription to continue after it ends.
Limits

What the demo does not show.

Not built yet

Proposed additions

The MOL card app, the odometer photo at clock-in, the ZKTeco email import, and the tablet's moving, idling and parked estimate are proposals. The demo shows the standard Odoo screens they would feed.

Not installed

Apps left out

Accounting and vendor bills, Documents and Helpdesk are available in Odoo 20 but not switched on here. Phone and WhatsApp are installed but not connected to any provider, so they cannot place calls or send messages.

Checked how

Verified by data, not by eye

Record counts and warning flags were confirmed through the programming interface. Walk through each script once on screen before presenting.
